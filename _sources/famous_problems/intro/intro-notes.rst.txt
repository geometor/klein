Prefaces & Introduction — Critical & Symbolic Notes
===================================================

.. rubric:: Philological Provenance, Field-Theoretic Formalization, and Sister-Corpus Concordance

Textual & Copyright Provenance (1895 / 1897 Witnesses)
------------------------------------------------------

* **Pure Public-Domain Witnesses:** Both the German *editio princeps* (:cite:`Teubner, 1895 <klein:1895>`, v + 66 S.) and the authorized first English translation by :person:`W. W. Beman <beman>` and :person:`D. E. Smith <smith>` (:cite:`Ginn & Co., 1897 <klein:1897>`, ix + 80 pp.) are strictly public-domain nineteenth-century texts. Later twentieth-century reprints (1930 Stechert / 1956 Dover) appended supplementary bibliographic notes by R. C. Archibald; this GEOMETOR codex excludes all post-1930 additions and restores the pure 1895/1897 treatises paired with original exact SymPy verifiers.
* **Chicago 1893 & Göttingen 1895 Nexus:** Beman and Smith's 1897 Preface highlights :person:`Klein's <klein>` 1893 visit to the Chicago World's Fair Mathematical Congress and Evanston Colloquium—the very same year :person:`M. W. Haskell <haskell>` published the authorized English translation of Klein's :cite:`*Erlangen Program* <klein:1893:erlangen>` (:ref:`Book 2 of this Codex <ep-index>`).

Algebraic Formalization in ``geometor.klein``
---------------------------------------------

Let $K_0 = \mathbb{Q}(a_1, \dots, a_r)$ denote the ground :term:`domain of rationality <domain-of-rationality>` generated by the coordinates of the given configuration. Klein's *Hauptsatz* states that a coordinate $x \in \mathbb{C}$ is straightedge-and-compass constructible over $K_0$ if and only if there exists a finite tower of quadratic field extensions

.. math::

   K_0 \subset K_1 \subset K_2 \subset \dots \subset K_m, \qquad K_j = K_{j-1}\!\left(\sqrt{\rho_{j-1}}\right), \quad \rho_{j-1} \in K_{j-1},

such that $x \in K_m$ (with real intermediate radicands $\rho_{j-1} > 0$ when tracking real planar intersections). In :func:`geometor.klein.constructibility.analyze_constructibility`, this criterion is tested symbolically over $\mathbb{Q}$ without a single floating-point operation.

Cross-Corpus Links Across GEOMETOR
----------------------------------

* **Euclidean Postulates (`geometor/euclid` & `geometor/elements`):** Euclid's Postulates 1–2 (straightedge) generate rational field operations via Book VI, Prop. 12 (fourth proportional), while Postulate 3 (compass) generates square-root extractions via Book II, Prop. 14 and Book VI, Prop. 13 (mean proportional).
* **Julius Petersen (`geometor/petersen`):** Klein's explicit tribute on p. 2 / S. 2 points directly to :person:`Petersen's <petersen>` :cite:`*Methods and Theories* (1879) <petersen:1879>` and *Theorie der algebraischen Gleichungen* (1878).
* **Hilbert's Gauge-Compass Refinement (`geometor/hilbert`):** Four years after Klein's Göttingen lectures, :person:`David Hilbert <hilbert>` (:cite:`*Grundlagen der Geometrie*, 1899, Ch. VII, §§ 36–39 <hilbert:1899>`) refined Klein's square-root tower by characterizing the *Pythagorean* sub-tower ($\sqrt{1 + \omega^2}$) constructible with straightedge and a fixed unit segment-transferrer (*Eichmaß*) alone.
* **Erlangen Program Connection:** In terms of :ref:`Erlangen § 1 <ep-sec-01-02-principal-hierarchy-en>`, theoretical constructibility is an invariant property under the :term:`Principal Group <principal-group>` of similarities, since isotropic rescaling, rotation, and translation leave coordinate ratios in $K_0$ unchanged.
