Part I, Chapter V — Analytical Summary
======================================

.. rubric:: Beyond Compass and Straightedge: Origami, Conics, Cissoid, Conchoid, and Linkages (§§ 1–5)

Hierarchy of Constructive Auxiliaries
-------------------------------------

Having completed the positive and negative theory of quadratic radical towers (Chapters I–IV), Klein concludes Part I by surveying algebraic means that transcend degree :math:`2^h`:

1. **Paper Folding (§ 1):** :person:`Hermann Wiener <wiener>` (polyhedral nets) and :person:`T. Sundara Row <sundara-row>` (:cite:`1893 <sundara-row:1893>`) introduce crease-based point and tangent constructions.
2. **Conic Intersections (§ 2):** Substituting :math:`y = x^2` reduces any cubic :math:`x^3 + ax^2 + bx + c = 0` or quartic :math:`x^4 + ax^3 + bx^2 + cx + d = 0` to intersecting the fixed parabola :math:`y = x^2` with a hyperbola (:math:`3` finite intersections + :math:`1` point at infinity for cubics; :math:`4` finite intersections for quartics).
3. **Cissoid of Diocles (§ 3):** The rational cuspidal cubic :math:`(x^2 + y^2)x - y^2 = 0` (:math:`r = \frac{\sin^2\theta}{\cos\theta}`) satisfies :math:`\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)^3 = \frac{y}{1 - x}`, converting the intercept :math:`2` on :math:`x = 0` directly into the cube-duplication ordinate :math:`\sqrt[3]{2}` on :math:`x = 1`.
4. **Conchoid of Nicomedes & Bézout Accounting (§ 4):** The circular quartic :math:`(x^2 + y^2)(x - a)^2 - b^2 x^2 = 0` trisects :math:`\angle MOY = \varphi`. Because the trisection cubic is irreducible, any algebraic curve intersection must yield all :math:`3` roots simultaneously: out of :math:`4 \times 2 = 8` Bézout intersections with the circle, :math:`2` lie at the origin node :math:`O`, :math:`2` at the circular points at infinity, and :math:`1` at the rational point :math:`B` on ray :math:`OM`, leaving **exact** :math:`3` **trisecting roots** :math:`\frac{\varphi + 2k\pi}{3}` (:math:`k = 0, 1, 2`).
5. **Continuous-Motion Trammels (§ 5):** Theoretical exactness with higher curves requires continuous mechanical generation (such as Nicomedes' conchoid trammel), not discrete point-plotting.
