Part I, Chapter I — Critical & Symbolic Notes
=============================================

.. rubric:: Galois-Theoretic Refinement of § 15 and Exact Quartic Resolvent Tests in ``geometor.klein``

Why Klein's Independence Hypothesis (§ 4) Is Essential
------------------------------------------------------

Klein's proof of § 10 (:math:`A + B Q_\mu = 0 \implies A = B = 0`) hinges directly on § 4: no :math:`Q_k` may lie in the field generated by the remaining radicals. Consider, for instance, a hidden dependency:

.. math::

   Q_1 = \sqrt{2}, \quad Q_2 = \sqrt{3}, \quad Q_3 = \sqrt{6} = Q_1 Q_2.

If one failed to eliminate :math:`Q_3 = Q_1 Q_2`, toggling the sign of :math:`Q_3` independently of :math:`Q_1, Q_2` would turn :math:`x = Q_1 Q_2 - Q_3 = 0` into :math:`2\sqrt{6} \neq 0`, violating conjugate invariance under :math:`f(x) = x = 0`! In :ref:`Chapter IV, § 6 <fp-part1-ch4-heptadecagon-en>`, Klein returns explicitly to § 4 when simplifying :person:`Gauss's <gauss>` :math:`17`-gon tower: among the four radicals :math:`\sqrt{17}`, :math:`\sqrt{x_1^2 + 4}`, :math:`\sqrt{x_2^2 + 4}`, and :math:`\sqrt{y_1^2 - 4y_4}`, the identity

.. math::

   \sqrt{x_1^2 + 4}\,\sqrt{x_2^2 + 4} = 2\sqrt{17}

shows that :math:`\sqrt{x_2^2 + 4}` is rationally dependent upon :math:`\sqrt{17}` and :math:`\sqrt{x_1^2 + 4}`, reducing :math:`m` from :math:`4` to :math:`4` independent radicals only after accounting for :math:`\sqrt{y_1^2 - 4y_4}` (so :math:`2^4 = 16 = \deg \Phi_{17}`).

Modern Galois Criterion vs. Degree :math:`2^h` (§ 15)
-----------------------------------------------------

In modern field theory (:person:`Évariste Galois <galois>`), if :math:`\varphi(x) \in K_0[x]` is irreducible with splitting field :math:`L` over :math:`K_0`:

* **Necessity of** :math:`\deg \varphi = 2^h`: Tower multiplicativity :math:`[K_m : K_0] = 2^m = [K_m : K_0(x_1)]\,[K_0(x_1) : K_0]` immediately gives :math:`\deg \varphi = [K_0(x_1) : K_0] = 2^h`.
* **Exact Necessary & Sufficient Condition:** :math:`x_1` is constructible by square roots over :math:`K_0` if and only if the degree :math:`[L : K_0] = |\mathrm{Gal}(L / K_0)|` of the **normal closure** :math:`L` is a power of :math:`2` (a finite :math:`2`-group).

For degree :math:`M = 4 = 2^2`, let :math:`\varphi(x) = x^4 + p x^2 + q x + r \in \mathbb{Q}[x]` be a depressed irreducible quartic. In :func:`geometor.klein.constructibility.cubic_resolvent` and :func:`~geometor.klein.constructibility.analyze_constructibility`, we form the exact cubic resolvent

.. math::

   R(t) = t^3 - 2p\,t^2 + (p^2 - 4r)\,t + q^2 \in \mathbb{Q}[t].

* **Constructible** :math:`D_4 / V_4 / C_4` **Quartic**: For :math:`\varphi(x) = x^4 - 2` (:math:`p = q = 0, r = -2`), :math:`R(t) = t^3 + 8t = t(t^2 + 8)` has the rational root :math:`t = 0`, so :math:`|\mathrm{Gal}| = 8 = 2^3` and :math:`x = \sqrt{\sqrt{2}}` is constructible (tower depth :math:`2`).
* **Non-Constructible** :math:`S_4` **Quartic**: For :math:`\varphi(x) = x^4 - x - 1` (:math:`p = 0, q = -1, r = -1`), :math:`\deg \varphi = 4 = 2^2`, yet :math:`R(t) = t^3 + 4t + 1` is irreducible over :math:`\mathbb{Q}` (:math:`R(\pm 1) \neq 0`), so :math:`\mathrm{Gal}(\varphi) \cong S_4` of order :math:`24 = 2^3 \cdot 3`—verified symbolically as **non-constructible** by ``analyze_constructibility(x**4 - x - 1)``!
