§ 7: Lie's Sphere Geometry & Contact Transformations — Critical Notes
=====================================================================

.. rubric:: Pentaspherical Coordinates, Lie Line-Sphere Duality, and Contact Jets

Lie's Line-Sphere Transformation
--------------------------------

In § 7, Klein celebrates :person:`Sophus Lie's <lie>` discovery that line geometry in :math:`\mathbb{P}^3` and sphere geometry in :math:`\mathbb{R}^3` are isomorphic manifestations of the same 6-variable quadratic projective geometry:

* **Pentaspherical Coordinates:** A sphere of radius :math:`R` and center :math:`(x_0, y_0, z_0)` is represented by 5 homogeneous coordinates :math:`(x_0 : y_0 : z_0 : \frac{x_0^2 + y_0^2 + z_0^2 - R^2 - 1}{2} : \frac{x_0^2 + y_0^2 + z_0^2 - R^2 + 1}{2i})`.
* **Lie Contact Duality:** Under Lie's transformation, straight lines map to spheres, and intersecting lines map to tangent spheres!
