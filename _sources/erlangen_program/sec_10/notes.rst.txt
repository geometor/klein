§ 10 & Concluding Remarks — Critical & Symbolic Notes
=====================================================

.. rubric:: The Galois–Erlangen Bridge Uniting Both Book Codices of Felix Klein

The Concluding Galois Parallel — Uniting Both Books of ``geometor.klein``
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

Klein's *Concluding Remarks* provide the exact architectural keystone uniting **Book 2** (*The Erlangen Program*, 1872/1893) and **Book 1** (*Famous Problems of Elementary Geometry*, 1895/1897):

* In **Galois Theory** (Book 1, Part I, Chs. I–IV), a polynomial :math:`f(x) = 0` over a :term:`domain of rationality <domain-of-rationality>` :math:`K` is governed by its finite Galois group :math:`\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)`. Subgroups :math:`H_k` cutting down :math:`\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)` correspond to intermediate resolvent expressions (such as :person:`Gauss's <gauss>` :math:`8`-, :math:`4`-, and :math:`2`-periods :math:`\eta_0, \eta'_0, \eta''_0` for :math:`p = 17`, where :math:`\mathbb{Z}_{16} \supset \mathbb{Z}_{8} \supset \mathbb{Z}_4 \supset \mathbb{Z}_2 \supset \{1\}`).
* In **Erlangen Geometry** (Book 2, §§ 1–2 & § 10), a manifold :math:`M` under a continuous group :math:`G` is governed by nested stabilizers :math:`G \supset H_1 \supset H_2 \dots` fixing distinguished geometric loci (such as the plane at infinity and :term:`the Absolute <the-absolute>` cutting :math:`\operatorname{PGL}_4` down to the Euclidean :term:`Principal Group <principal-group>`).
* Figures admitting nontrivial symmetry subgroups :math:`\operatorname{Stab}_G(F) \neq \{1\}`—such as the regular polyhedra (*Vorlesungen über das Ikosaeder*, 1884, cited in 1893 fn. 40), surfaces of revolution, and Klein–Lie :math:`W`-curves—are the exact geometric counterparts of special equations with abelian or solvable Galois groups!
