§ 6. Geometry of Reciprocal Radii (Möbius Inversion) & Complex Variables — Summary
==================================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Synopsis of Conformal Möbius Geometry and Binary Forms

§ 6. Geometry of Reciprocal Radii (Möbius Inversion) & Complex Binary Forms
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. **Structural Parallel Between Projective and Inversion Geometry:**
   Whereas projective geometry adjoins a *plane* (or line) at infinity and treats circles/spheres as special conics/quadrics relative to the imaginary circle at infinity, the :term:`geometry of reciprocal radii <reciprocal-radii>` (:person:`Möbius <moebius>` inversion) adjoins a **single point at infinity** :math:`\infty` and treats straight lines and planes as circles and spheres passing through :math:`\infty`. Fixing :math:`\infty` immediately recovers the Euclidean :term:`Principal Group <principal-group>`.
2. **Projective Quadric Model:**
   Stereographic projection identifies :math:`2`-dimensional inversion geometry with projective geometry on a smooth quadric surface in :math:`\mathbb{P}^3`, and :math:`3`-dimensional inversion geometry with projective geometry on a :math:`3`-quadric in :math:`\mathbb{P}^4` (:math:`5` homogeneous variables).
3. **Complex Variable** :math:`z = x + iy` **& the Riemann Sphere**:
   In the real plane, fractional linear transformations :math:`z' = \frac{\alpha z + \beta}{\gamma z + \delta}` (together with anti-conformal :math:`\bar{z}` inversions, added by Klein in 1893 fn. 26) generate the full planar Möbius group, identifying complex binary invariant theory with real projective geometry on the Riemann sphere.
