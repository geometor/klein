§ 6. Geometry of Reciprocal Radii (Möbius Inversion) & Complex Variables — Summary
==================================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Synopsis of Conformal Möbius Geometry and Binary Forms

§ 6. Geometry of Reciprocal Radii (Möbius Inversion) & Complex Binary Forms
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. **Structural Parallel Between Projective and Inversion Geometry:**
   Whereas projective geometry adjoins a *plane* (or line) at infinity and treats circles/spheres as special conics/quadrics relative to the imaginary circle at infinity, the :term:`geometry of reciprocal radii <reciprocal-radii>` (:person:`Möbius <moebius>` inversion) adjoins a **single point at infinity** $\infty$ and treats straight lines and planes as circles and spheres passing through $\infty$. Fixing $\infty$ immediately recovers the Euclidean :term:`Principal Group <principal-group>`.
2. **Projective Quadric Model:**
   Stereographic projection identifies $2$-dimensional inversion geometry with projective geometry on a smooth quadric surface in $\mathbb{P}^3$, and $3$-dimensional inversion geometry with projective geometry on a $3$-quadric in $\mathbb{P}^4$ ($5$ homogeneous variables).
3. **Complex Variable $z = x + iy$ & the Riemann Sphere:**
   In the real plane, fractional linear transformations $z' = \frac{\alpha z + \beta}{\gamma z + \delta}$ (together with anti-conformal $\bar{z}$ inversions, added by Klein in 1893 fn. 26) generate the full planar Möbius group, identifying complex binary invariant theory with real projective geometry on the Riemann sphere.
