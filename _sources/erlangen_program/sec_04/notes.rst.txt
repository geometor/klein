.. rubric:: The Isomorphism :math:`\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb{C}) \cong \operatorname{PO}_3(\mathbb{C})` and Geometric Transfer

Classical Lie-Group Isomorphisms Discovered in § 4
--------------------------------------------------

Klein's geometric transference arguments establish low-dimensional exceptional isomorphisms of classical Lie algebras and algebraic groups without matrix computations:

* :math:`\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb{C}) \;\cong\; \operatorname{PO}_3(\mathbb{C})`:
  Via the Veronese conic embedding :math:`[u : v] \mapsto [u^2 : uv : v^2]`, binary projective transformations act as projective orthogonal transformations preserving the conic :math:`x z - y^2 = 0`, identifying binary invariant theory with planar non-Euclidean Cayley–Klein geometry.
* **Stereographic Projection of the Quadric:**
  Stereographic projection of a quadric surface from a point :math:`P_0` identifies planar similarity geometry with projective quadric geometry fixing :math:`P_0`.
