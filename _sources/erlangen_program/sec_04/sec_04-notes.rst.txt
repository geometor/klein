§ 4: Representation & Lie Group Isomorphisms — Critical & Symbolic Notes
========================================================================

.. rubric:: The Isomorphism $\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb{C}) \cong \operatorname{PO}_3(\mathbb{C})$ and Geometric Transfer

Classical Lie-Group Isomorphisms Discovered in § 4
--------------------------------------------------

Klein's geometric transference arguments establish low-dimensional exceptional isomorphisms of classical Lie algebras and algebraic groups without matrix computations:

* **$\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb{C}) \;\cong\; \operatorname{PO}_3(\mathbb{C})$:**
  Via the Veronese conic embedding $[u : v] \mapsto [u^2 : uv : v^2]$, binary projective transformations act as projective orthogonal transformations preserving the conic $x z - y^2 = 0$, identifying binary invariant theory with planar non-Euclidean Cayley–Klein geometry.
* **Stereographic Projection of the Quadric:**
  Stereographic projection of a quadric surface from a point $P_0$ identifies planar similarity geometry with projective quadric geometry fixing $P_0$.
