§ 5. Choice of Space-Element & Hesse's Principle — Summary
==========================================================

.. rubric:: Dimensionality as Relative to the Space-Element & Dedekind Bodies

§ 5. Arbitrariness of the Space-Element and Transitive Group-Bodies (*Körper*)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Dimension Is Relative to the Space-Element:** Taking points (:math:`\infty^3`), lines (:math:`\infty^4`, :person:`Plücker <pluecker>`), planes (:math:`\infty^3`), or general quadrics (:math:`\infty^9`) as the primary :term:`space-element <space-element>` changes the dimension of space at will, while **preserving the mathematical substance of the geometry** so long as the underlying transformation group remains unchanged.
* **Hesse's Principle & Plücker's Line Quadric:** Pairing points on a conic identifies binary form theory with Cayley–Klein planar metric geometry; similarly, Plücker's :math:`6` homogeneous line coordinates satisfying :math:`p_{01} p_{23} + p_{02} p_{31} + p_{03} p_{12} = 0` identify :math:`3`-dimensional projective geometry (:math:`\operatorname{PGL}_4 \rtimes \mathbb{Z}_2`) with :math:`5`-dimensional projective metric geometry relative to the Klein quadric in :math:`\mathbb{P}^5`.
* **Group-Bodies (*Körper*):** Borrowing Dedekind's algebraic term *Körper*, Klein defines a geometric :term:`body <body-group>` as a single transitive orbit :math:`G \cdot x_0` (requiring in 1893 that the stabilizer contain no normal subgroup of :math:`G`). Coherent coordinate representation demands that chosen space-elements form a single *Körper* (homogeneous space) or decompose cleanly into *Körper*.
