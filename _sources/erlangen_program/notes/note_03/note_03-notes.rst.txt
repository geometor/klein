Note III: Value of Space-Perception (*Anschauung*) — Critical Notes
===================================================================

.. rubric:: The Autonomous Epistemic Value of Spatial Models and Intuition

Geometric Models as Objects of Study (*Anschauliche Geometrie*)
---------------------------------------------------------------

While abstract mathematics treats spatial figures merely as illustrative diagrams, Klein establishes the epistemic legitimacy of an autonomous *intuitive geometry*:

* **Physical Models:** In Göttingen, Klein created a world-renowned collection of plaster and string models of algebraic surfaces (singular cubic surfaces, Kummer surfaces).
* **Perception as Subject:** A vividly visualized or physically constructed model is not merely a pedagogical aid, but the actual object of geometric knowledge.
