§ 8: Birational Geometry & Analysis Situs — Critical & Symbolic Notes
=====================================================================

.. rubric:: Infinite Continuous Transformation Groups and Algebraic Curve Invariants

Infinite Groups in Geometry (§ 8)
---------------------------------

In § 8, Klein steps beyond finite-parameter Lie groups to infinite-dimensional transformation groups:

* **Birational Transformations (Cremona Group):**
  Rational transformations of algebraic curves and surfaces. Invariants include the curve genus $p = g$, the canonical linear series $|K|$, and moduli of curves (Riemann, Clebsch, Max Noether).
* **Analysis Situs (Topology):**
  Continuous 1-to-1 deformations (homeomorphisms) preserving connectivity, boundary relations, and topological Euler characteristic $\chi = 2 - 2g$.
