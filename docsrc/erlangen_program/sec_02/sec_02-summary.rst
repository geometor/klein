=======================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Structure of Group Containment and Distinguished Fundamental Loci

§ 2. Stabilizer Subgroups and Distinguished Fundamental Loci
------------------------------------------------------------

Klein establishes the Galois-style duality between **restricting the group** and **adjoining a fixed geometric locus**:

* **Stabilizer Principle (Descending to a Subgroup** :math:`H \subset G`):
  Studying configurations on :math:`M` relative to a fixed figure :math:`\Omega` under a larger group :math:`G` is mathematically identical to studying configurations *by themselves* under the stabilizer subgroup

  .. math::

     \operatorname{Stab}_G(\Omega) \;=\; \{\, g \in G \mid g(\Omega) = \Omega \,\}.

  For example, spherical trigonometry is the invariant theory of the :math:`3`-parameter rotation subgroup fixing a center :math:`O` inside the Principal Group.
* **Ascent to Supergroups** (:math:`G_{\text{sim}} \subset G`):
  Replacing the Principal Group :math:`G_{\text{sim}}` by a larger group :math:`G` (such as affine, projective, or inversion transformations) retains only a subset of elementary invariants as *intrinsic* properties; all remaining elementary metric properties reappear as *joint invariants* of the figure together with a distinguished locus :math:`\Omega` whose stabilizer in :math:`G` is :math:`G_{\text{sim}}`.
