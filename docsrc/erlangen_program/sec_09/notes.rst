.. rubric:: Contact Jets, Pfaffian Geometry, and Clebsch's Connexes

Jet-Bundle Hierarchy: Contact, Point, and Projective Groups (§ 9)
-----------------------------------------------------------------

At the close of § 9 (crediting :person:`Sophus Lie <lie>` and :person:`Alfred Clebsch <clebsch>`), Klein formulates a remarkably modern jet-manifold characterization of the three overarching transformation hierarchies on :math:`3`-space:

* **Contact-Transformations:** Preserve incidence :math:`dz - p\,dx - q\,dy = 0` of consecutive **surface-elements** :math:`(x, y, z, p, q) \in \mathbb{P}(T^*\mathbb{R}^3)` (:math:`5`-manifold).
* **Point-Transformations (Diffeomorphisms):** Preserve united position of consecutive **line-elements** :math:`(x, y, z, [dx : dy : dz]) \in \mathbb{P}(T\mathbb{R}^3)` (:math:`5`-manifold).
* **Projective & Dualistic Transformations** (:math:`\operatorname{PGL}_4 \rtimes \mathbb{Z}_2`): Preserve second-order united position of consecutive **connex-elements** (incident point–line–plane flags :math:`(p \in \ell \subset \Pi)` in the :math:`6`-dimensional flag manifold :math:`\operatorname{Fl}(1, 2, 3; 4)`).
