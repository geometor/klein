§ 9. Sophus Lie's Group of All Contact-Transformations — Summary
================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Synopsis of Contact Geometry and Clebsch's Connexes

§ 9. Sophus Lie's Group of All Contact-Transformations
------------------------------------------------------

* Under general :term:`contact-transformations <contact-transformation>` of :math:`\mathbb{R}^3`, points, curves, and surfaces mix into one another; hence points do *not* form a transitive :term:`body <body-group>`.
* Introducing the :math:`5`-dimensional space of **surface-elements** :math:`(x, y, z, p, q)` with :math:`p = \partial z / \partial x`, :math:`q = \partial z / \partial y`, contact-transformations are precisely diffeomorphisms preserving the Pfaffian contact equation

  .. math::

     dz - p\,dx - q\,dy \;=\; 0.

* Points, curves, and surfaces become uniform :math:`2`-dimensional Legendrian submanifolds of united surface-elements, and every first-order PDE :math:`F(x, y, z, p, q) = 0` becomes locally equivalent to every other under contact transformations.
