Note II: Division of Modern Geometry into Theories — Critical Notes
===================================================================

.. rubric:: Overcoming Fragmentation Between Mathematical Physics and Geometry

The Unity of Geometric Disciplines
----------------------------------

Klein laments the mutual isolation of three great branches of nineteenth-century mathematics:

1. **Mathematical Physics:** Frequently ignored the power of projective coordinate systems and duality.
2. **Projective Geometry:** Overlooked the profound metric results of differential geometry (Gauss's curvature of surfaces).
3. **Differential Geometry:** Developed without systematic reference to transformation groups.

The Erlangen Program demonstrates that all three are manifestations of group-theoretic invariance on smooth manifolds.
