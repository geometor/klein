Note I: Synthetic vs. Analytic Method — Critical Notes
======================================================

.. rubric:: The Synthesis of Poncelet, Steiner, Plücker, and Clebsch

Reconciling Synthesis and Analysis in Projective Geometry
---------------------------------------------------------

In Note I, Klein addresses the sharp nineteenth-century division between:

* **Synthetic Geometers (:person:`Poncelet <poncelet>`, :person:`Steiner <steiner>`, :person:`von Staudt <staudt>`):** Relied on pure spatial intuition, perspective, and harmonic quadruples, viewing algebraic coordinates as extraneous.
* **Analytic Geometers (:person:`Plücker <pluecker>`, :person:`Hesse <hesse>`, :person:`Clebsch <clebsch>`):** Deployed homogeneous coordinates and algebraic invariant theory.

Klein insists this antithesis is obsolete: algebraic formulas provide a transparent calculus that runs ahead of thought, but no investigation is mathematically complete until it becomes intuitive (*anschaulich*).
