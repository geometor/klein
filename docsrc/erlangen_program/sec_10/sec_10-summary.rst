===================================================================================

.. rubric:: Analytical Synopsis of :math:`n`-Manifolds and the Continuous-Discrete Galois Bridge

§ 10 & Concluding Remarks: :math:`n`-Manifolds, Coordinate-Free Invariants, and Galois Theory
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* :math:`n`-**Manifolds (§ 10**): Projective invariant theory (:math:`\operatorname{PGL}_{n+1}`), constant-curvature Riemannian/Cayley–Klein manifolds (stabilizing a quadric), and flat Euclidean manifolds (stabilizing adegenerate hyperplane-quadric pair) unify differential and projective geometry in arbitrary dimension.
* **Coordinate-Free Invariant Formalism:** Coordinates are harmless only when the analytical calculus operates exclusively with group invariants (such as algebraic invariants or Hamilton's quaternions for :math:`\operatorname{SO}_3`).
* **The Bridge to Book 1 — Continuous vs. Discrete Galois Theory:** In his closing manifesto, Klein explicitly parallels **continuous transformation groups** on manifolds with **Évariste Galois's permutation groups** on polynomial roots—linking the Erlangen Program directly to the algebraic constructibility and icosahedral/cyclotomic group theory of *Famous Problems*!
