§ 3: Projective Geometry & The Absolute Conic — Critical & Symbolic Notes
=========================================================================

.. rubric:: Group Extensions, The Conic at Infinity, and French Synthetic Precursors

* **Group Inclusions & Projective Extension:**
  The projective group :math:`\operatorname{PGL}_4(\mathbb{R})` (:math:`15` parameters) encompasses the :math:`7`-parameter principal group :math:`\operatorname{Sim}(\mathbb{R}^3)` by adjoining transformations that move the plane at infinity :math:`\Pi_\infty` to finite positions.
* **Cayley's Absolute:**
  Metric Euclidean geometry is recovered by adjoining to :math:`\operatorname{PGL}_4` the invariant specification of the plane at infinity and :term:`the Absolute <the-absolute>` (the degenerate imaginary circle at infinity :math:`\Omega_\infty`).

* **Footnote 14 (1872** *Chasles* :math:`\to` **1893** *[the French school]*\ **):**
  In the 1872 Erlangen pamphlet (fn. 13), Klein attributed the interpretation of metric properties via the imaginary spherical circle at infinity solely to :person:`Michel Chasles <chasles>`. Recognizing the earlier foundational priority of :person:`Jean-Victor Poncelet <poncelet>` (:cite:`1822 <poncelet:1822>`) and Edmond Laguerre (1853, angle via logarithm of cross-ratio with :math:`I, J`), Klein broadened the 1893 wording to ``[the French school]``.
* **Pappus & Projective Cross-Ratio (`geometor/pappus`):** See :cite:`Pappus, *Collection* Book VII <pappus:collection>` for the ancient root of projective invariance and :person:`von Staudt's <staudt>` *Geometrie der Lage* discussed in § 3.
