§ 4. Transfer of Properties by Representation (*Abbildung*) — Summary
=====================================================================

.. rubric:: Group Isomorphisms Inducing Geometric Equivalences

§ 4. Transfer of Properties by Representation (*Abbildung*)
-----------------------------------------------------------

Whenever a bijection :math:`f : A \to A'` conjugates a group action :math:`(A, B)` into :math:`(A', B' = f B f^{-1})`, the two geometries :math:`(A, B)` and :math:`(A', B')` are **isomorphic**. Klein illustrates this by two canonical isomorphisms:

1. **Binary Forms** :math:`\cong` **Conic Projectivities**: Stereographic projection from a point on a conic maps :math:`\mathbb{P}^1` under :math:`\operatorname{PGL}_2` isomorphically onto the conic under planar projectivities stabilizing it.
2. **Elementary Plane Geometry** :math:`\cong` **Punctured Quadric**: Stereographic projection of a quadric surface from a point :math:`P_0` maps the two generators through :math:`P_0` to the circular points :math:`I, J` at infinity, identifying planar similarity geometry with projective quadric geometry fixing :math:`P_0`.
