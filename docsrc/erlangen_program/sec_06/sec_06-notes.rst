§ 6: Conformal Inversion & Riemann's $x+iy$ — Critical & Symbolic Notes
=======================================================================

.. rubric:: Conformal Möbius Geometry, Tetracyclic Coordinates, and Liouville's Theorem

Liouville's Conformal Rigidity Theorem
--------------------------------------

In § 6, Klein highlights the geometry of reciprocal radii:

* **Conformal Action:** The group of geometric inversion in the plane preserves angles (is conformal).
* **Riemann Sphere Interpretation:** Stereographic projection identifies Möbius transformations of the complex plane with the projective group $\operatorname{PSL}_2(\mathbb{C})$ acting on the Riemann sphere $\mathbb{C}\mathbb{P}^1$.
* **Higher Dimensions:** By :person:`Liouville's <liouville>` celebrated theorem (1850), in dimensions $n \ge 3$ the *only* conformal transformations are compositions of Euclidean similarities and inversions (the Möbius group $\operatorname{SO}^+(1, n+1)$).
