.. _bibliography:

Bibliography & Cited Sources
============================

.. rubric:: Primary Klein Editions, Historical Treatises, and GEOMETOR Sister Corpora

This bibliography catalogues **36 primary editions, classical memoirs, and sister-corpus treatises** referenced across the dual 4-track Book Codices. Every inline ``:cite:`` role resolves directly to its corresponding entry below.

----

.. _cite-klein-1895:

Felix Klein (ausgearbeitet von F. Tägert) — Vorträge über ausgewählte Fragen der Elementargeometrie (1895)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``klein:1895``
* **Publication / Locus:** Leipzig: B. G. Teubner (v + 66 S., 10 Fig., 2 lithogr. Tafeln)
* **Context:** First German edition; Festschrift prepared for the Elberfeld Oberlehrer gathering from Klein's Göttingen Easter 1895 holiday course. Public domain.

.. _cite-klein-1897:

Felix Klein (trans. W. W. Beman & D. E. Smith) — Famous Problems of Elementary Geometry: The Duplication of the Cube, The Trisection of an Angle, The Quadrature of the Circle (1897)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``klein:1897``
* **Publication / Locus:** Boston and London: Ginn & Company (ix + 80 pp.)
* **Context:** Authorized first English translation from Teubner's 1895 German text (unencumbered by Archibald's 1930 annotations). Public domain.

.. _cite-klein-1872:

Felix Klein — Vergleichende Betrachtungen über neuere geometrische Forschungen (1872)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``klein:1872``
* **Publication / Locus:** Erlangen: Verlag von Andreas Deichert (48 S.); reprinted in Mathematische Annalen 43 (1893), pp. 63–100
* **Context:** Klein's inaugural Erlangen Program monograph classifying geometries as invariant theories of transformation groups. Public domain (Project Gutenberg #38033).

.. _cite-klein-1893-erlangen:

Felix Klein (trans. M. W. Haskell) — A Comparative Review of Recent Researches in Geometry (1893)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``klein:1893:erlangen``
* **Publication / Locus:** Bulletin of the New York Mathematical Society 2 (10), July 1893, pp. 215–249
* **Context:** Authorized English translation of the Erlangen Program with Klein's 1893 Prefatory Note. Public domain (arXiv:0807.3161).

.. _cite-klein-1871-noneuclid:

Felix Klein — Über die sogenannte Nicht-Euklidische Geometrie (1871–1873)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``klein:1871:noneuclid``
* **Publication / Locus:** Mathematische Annalen 4 (1871), pp. 573–625; 6 (1873), pp. 112–145
* **Context:** Established the projective Cayley–Klein models of hyperbolic, elliptic, and parabolic metric geometries; summarized in Erlangen Note V.

.. _cite-klein-1872-linien:

Felix Klein — Über Liniengeometrie und metrische Geometrie (1872)
-----------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``klein:1872:linien``
* **Publication / Locus:** Mathematische Annalen 5, pp. 257–277
* **Context:** Connected Plücker's 4D line quadric in P^5 with Lie's sphere geometry and non-Euclidean metric transformations (Erlangen §§ 5 & 7).

.. _cite-gauss-1801:

Carl Friedrich Gauss — Disquisitiones Arithmeticae, Sectio Septima: De aequationibus circuli sectiones definientibus (1801)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``gauss:1801``
* **Publication / Locus:** Leipzig: Gerhard Fleischer, §§ 335–366
* **Context:** Proved constructibility of regular polygons of Fermat-prime order p = 2^(2^mu) + 1 and gave the explicit period reduction for p = 17.

.. _cite-wantzel-1837:

Pierre Laurent Wantzel — Recherches sur les moyens de reconnaître si un problème de Géométrie peut se résoudre avec la règle et le compas (1837)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``wantzel:1837``
* **Publication / Locus:** Journal de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées (Liouville) 2, pp. 366–372
* **Context:** First complete algebraic proof of the degree-2^h necessity criterion, settling Delian duplication, angle trisection, and non-Fermat polygons.

.. _cite-eisenstein-1850:

Gotthold Eisenstein — Über die Irreductibilität und einige andere Eigenschaften der Gleichung, von welcher die Theilung der ganzen Lemniscate abhängt (1850)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``eisenstein:1850``
* **Publication / Locus:** Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik (Crelle) 39, pp. 160–179 (p. 167)
* **Context:** Source of Eisenstein's p-divisibility irreducibility criterion applied by Klein in Famous Problems Part I, Ch. III, §§ 7–8.

.. _cite-richelot-1832:

Friedrich Julius Richelot — De resolutione algebraica aequationis X^257 = 1, sive de divisione circuli per bisectionem anguli septies repetitam in partes 257 inter se aequales commentatio coronata (1832)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``richelot:1832``
* **Publication / Locus:** Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik (Crelle) 9, pp. 1–26, 146–161, 209–230, 337–357
* **Context:** Explicit cyclotomic resolution of the regular 257-gon (Famous Problems Part I, Ch. III, § 1).

.. _cite-hermes-1894:

Johann Gustav Hermes — Über die Teilung des Kreises in 65537 gleiche Teile (1894)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``hermes:1894``
* **Publication / Locus:** Nachrichten von der Kgl. Gesellschaft der Wissenschaften zu Göttingen, Math.-phys. Klasse 1894, Nr. 3, pp. 170–186
* **Context:** Summary of Hermes's ten-year period computation for the regular 65537-gon stored in Göttingen.

.. _cite-bachmann-1872:

Paul Bachmann — Die Lehre von der Kreistheilung und ihre Beziehungen zur Zahlentheorie (1872)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``bachmann:1872``
* **Publication / Locus:** Leipzig: B. G. Teubner
* **Context:** Standard cyclotomy treatise recommended by Klein in Famous Problems Part I, Ch. III–IV.

.. _cite-petersen-1879:

Julius Petersen — Methods and Theories for the Solution of Problems of Geometrical Constructions (and Theorie der algebraischen Gleichungen, 1878) (1879)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``petersen:1879``
* **Publication / Locus:** Copenhagen: Andr. Fred. Høst & Søn / London: Sampson Low
* **Context:** Praised by Klein in Famous Problems Introduction (p. 2) for elementary algebraic proofs of geometric constructibility (geometor/petersen).

.. _cite-lemoine-1902:

Émile Lemoine — Géométrographie, ou Art des constructions géométriques (1902)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``lemoine:1902``
* **Publication / Locus:** Paris: C. Naud (Collection Scientia No. 18)
* **Context:** Quantitative complexity calculus of compass-and-straightedge constructions complementing Klein's restricted-instrument hierarchy (geometor/lemoine).

.. _cite-mascheroni-1797:

Lorenzo Mascheroni — La Geometria del Compasso (1797)
-----------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``mascheroni:1797``
* **Publication / Locus:** Pavia: Eredi di Pietro Galeazzi
* **Context:** Proved compass-only constructibility of all Euclidean points (Famous Problems Part I, Ch. IV, § 7).

.. _cite-poncelet-1822:

Jean-Victor Poncelet — Traité des propriétés projectives des figures (1822)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``poncelet:1822``
* **Publication / Locus:** Paris: Bachelier
* **Context:** Foundational treatise on central projection, ideal elements, and single-circle straightedge constructions.

.. _cite-steiner-1833:

Jakob Steiner — Die geometrischen Konstruktionen, ausgeführt mittelst der geraden Linie und eines festen Kreises (1833)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``steiner:1833``
* **Publication / Locus:** Berlin: Ferdinand Dümmler
* **Context:** Proved sufficiency of straightedge + one fixed circle with known center for all quadratic constructions.

.. _cite-staudt-1842:

Karl Georg Christian von Staudt — Construction des regulären Siebzehnecks (1842)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``staudt:1842``
* **Publication / Locus:** Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik (Crelle) 24, p. 252 (also Geometrie der Lage, 1847)
* **Context:** Original single-circle straightedge construction of the regular 17-gon adapted by Schröter and Klein.

.. _cite-schroeter-1872:

Heinrich Schröter — Zur v. Staudtschen Construction des regulären Siebzehnecks (1872)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``schroeter:1872``
* **Publication / Locus:** Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik (Crelle) 75, pp. 13–24
* **Context:** Direct geometric model for Klein's fixed-circle quadratic period solver in Famous Problems Part I, Ch. IV, §§ 8–9.

.. _cite-sundara-row-1893:

T. Sundara Row — Geometrical Exercises in Paper Folding (1893)
--------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``sundara-row:1893``
* **Publication / Locus:** Madras: Addison & Co.
* **Context:** Cited by Klein in Famous Problems Part I, Ch. V, § 1 on origami constructions.

.. _cite-cantor-1874:

Georg Cantor — Über eine Eigenschaft des Inbegriffes aller reellen algebraischen Zahlen (and Über eine elementare Frage der Mannigfaltigkeitslehre, 1891) (1874 / 1891)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``cantor:1874``
* **Publication / Locus:** Crelle's Journal 77, pp. 258–262; Jahresbericht der DMV 1, pp. 75–78
* **Context:** Countability of algebraic numbers by height N and diagonal proof of uncountability of the continuum (Famous Problems Part II, Ch. I).

.. _cite-rudio-1892:

Ferdinand Rudio — Archimedes, Huygens, Lambert, Legendre. Vier Abhandlungen über die Kreismessung (1892)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``rudio:1892``
* **Publication / Locus:** Leipzig: B. G. Teubner
* **Context:** Historical source anthology underlying Famous Problems Part II, Ch. II.

.. _cite-hermite-1873:

Charles Hermite — Sur la fonction exponentielle (1873)
------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``hermite:1873``
* **Publication / Locus:** Comptes Rendus de l'Académie des Sciences 77, pp. 18–24, 74–79, 226–233, 285–293
* **Context:** First proof of the transcendence of Euler's constant e.

.. _cite-lindemann-1882:

Ferdinand Lindemann — Über die Zahl π (1882)
--------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``lindemann:1882``
* **Publication / Locus:** Mathematische Annalen 20, pp. 213–225
* **Context:** Proved the transcendence of pi and impossibility of squaring the circle via 1 + e^(i*pi) = 0.

.. _cite-gordan-1893:

Paul Gordan — Transcendenz von e und π (1893)
---------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``gordan:1893``
* **Publication / Locus:** Mathematische Annalen 43, pp. 222–224 ( alongside Hilbert pp. 216–219 & Hurwitz pp. 220–221)
* **Context:** Purely algebraic operator proof (h^r = r!) of Hermite's and Lindemann's theorems presented in Famous Problems Part II, Chs. III–IV.

.. _cite-abakanowicz-1889:

Bruno Abdank-Abakanowicz (trans. Emil Bitterli) — Die Integraphen: Die Integralkurve und ihre Anwendungen (1889)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``abakanowicz:1889``
* **Publication / Locus:** Leipzig: B. G. Teubner (French original: Paris, Gauthier-Villars, 1886)
* **Context:** Mechanical continuous-motion quadrature of the circle analyzed in Famous Problems Part II, Ch. V / Anhang.

.. _cite-cayley-1859:

Arthur Cayley — A Sixth Memoir upon Quantics (1859)
---------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``cayley:1859``
* **Publication / Locus:** Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society of London 149, pp. 61–90
* **Context:** Subsumed metrical geometry under projective invariants relative to the Absolute quadric (Erlangen §§ 1–2, § 10, Note V).

.. _cite-riemann-1854:

Bernhard Riemann — Über die Hypothesen, welche der Geometrie zu Grunde liegen (1854 (pub. 1867))
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``riemann:1854``
* **Publication / Locus:** Abhandlungen der Kgl. Gesellschaft der Wissenschaften zu Göttingen 13
* **Context:** Introduced n-dimensional manifolds and constant-curvature metrics contrasted and unified with projective group theory in Erlangen § 10 & Notes IV–VI.

.. _cite-lie-1872:

Sophus Lie — Über Complexe, insbesondere Linien- und Kugel-Complexe, mit Anwendung auf die Theorie partieller Differential-Gleichungen (and Theorie der Transformationsgruppen, 1888–1893) (1872)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``lie:1872``
* **Publication / Locus:** Mathematische Annalen 5, pp. 145–256
* **Context:** Lie's contact transformations and line-to-sphere correspondence featured in §§ 7 & 9 of the Erlangen Program.

.. _cite-euclid-elements:

Euclid (trans. Sir Thomas L. Heath) — The Thirteen Books of Euclid's Elements (c. 300 BC / 1908)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``euclid:elements``
* **Publication / Locus:** Cambridge University Press, Books I & IV
* **Context:** Classical straightedge-and-compass postulates and regular polygon constructions (geometor/euclid, geometor/elements).

.. _cite-kepler-1619:

Johannes Kepler — Harmonices Mundi, Liber I: De figuris regularibus harmonicis (1619)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``kepler:1619``
* **Publication / Locus:** Linz: Johann Planck (Props. XLV–XLVII)
* **Context:** Philosophico-geometric analysis of constructible ('knowable') vs. non-constructible ('unknowable') regular polygons including the heptagon (geometor/kepler).

.. _cite-hilbert-1899:

David Hilbert — Grundlagen der Geometrie (1899 / 1902)
------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``hilbert:1899``
* **Publication / Locus:** Leipzig: B. G. Teubner (Ch. VII, §§ 36–39)
* **Context:** Axiomatic characterization of problems solvable with straightedge and segment-transferrer (Eichmass) over Pythagorean vs. Euclidean fields (geometor/hilbert).

.. _cite-russell-1897:

Bertrand Russell — An Essay on the Foundations of Geometry (and The Principles of Mathematics, 1903) (1897 / 1903)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``russell:1897``
* **Publication / Locus:** Cambridge University Press
* **Context:** Philosophical and logicist reception of Klein's Cayley–Klein metageometry and Erlangen hierarchy (geometor/bertrand-russell).

.. _cite-hoenen-1954:

* **Citation Key:** ``hoenen:1954``

.. _cite-pappus-collection:

Pappus of Alexandria — Synagoge (Collection), Books III, IV & VII (c. 340 AD)

* **Citation Key:** ``pappus:collection``

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* **Citation Key:** ``wildberger:2005``
