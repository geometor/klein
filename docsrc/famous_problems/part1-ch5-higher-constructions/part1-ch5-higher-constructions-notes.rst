Part I, Chapter V — Critical & Symbolic Notes
=============================================

.. rubric:: Bibliographic Variants (Sundara Row 1893), Circular Points at Infinity, and Origami Cubics

Textual & Mathematical Commentary
---------------------------------

* **Sundara Row Citation (1895 DE vs. 1897 EN):** In 1895 DE (S. 33), Klein cited :person:`T. Sundara Row's <sundara-row>` book from memory as *„On paper folding“ (London, Macmillan, 1893)*. In preparing the 1897 English translation (p. 42), :person:`Beman <beman>` and :person:`Smith <smith>` procured a physical copy directly from India and restored the exact title and imprint: :cite:`*Geometrical Exercises in Paper Folding* (Madras: Addison & Co., 1893) <sundara-row:1893>`—subsequently editing the famous Open Court American edition of 1901! (Modern axiomatization of paper folding via the Huzita–Justin axioms proves that single-fold common tangents to two parabolas solve arbitrary cubic equations, including $\sqrt[3]{2}$ and angle trisection.)
* **Projective Role of the Circular Points at Infinity (`geometor/pappus` & Erlangen Program):** Notice how effortlessly Klein uses projective algebraic geometry in §§ 3–4: homogenizing $(x^2 + y^2)x - y^2 z = 0$ and $(x^2 + y^2)(x - az)^2 - b^2 x^2 z^2 = 0$ at $z = 0$ gives $(x^2 + y^2)x = 0$ and $(x^2 + y^2)x^2 = 0$, showing that both the :term:`cissoid <cissoid>` and the :term:`conchoid <conchoid>` pass through :person:`Poncelet's <poncelet>` imaginary circular points $(1 : \pm i : 0)$—the very :term:`Absolute <the-absolute>` of Euclidean metric geometry highlighted in :ref:`Erlangen Program Note V <ep-notes-i-vii-en>`!
* **Ancient Classification of Problems (`geometor/pappus`):** Klein's separation of straightedge-and-compasses constructions (Chapters I–IV), conic-section solid constructions (§ 2), and higher-curve *linear* constructions (§§ 3–5) mirrors :person:`Pappus's <pappus>` famous tripartite classification (*plane*, *solid*, and *linear* problems) in :cite:`*Collection*, Book III & Book IV <pappus:collection>`, now grounded in exact Galois degree and Bézout intersection multiplicity.
