Prefaces & Introduction — Analytical Summary
============================================

.. rubric:: Structural Synopsis of Klein's Göttingen Easter 1895 Programme and Algebraic Criterion

Historical Occasion & Pedagogical Mission
-----------------------------------------

At Easter 1895, :person:`Felix Klein <klein>` delivered a holiday lecture series at Göttingen for secondary-school mathematics and physics teachers (*Oberlehrer*). Redacted by :person:`Friedrich Tägert <taegert>` as a *Festschrift* for the Whitsuntide 1895 Göttingen convention of the *Verein zur Förderung des mathematischen und naturwissenschaftlichen Unterrichts* (:cite:`Leipzig: Teubner, 1895 <klein:1895>`) and translated into English two years later by :person:`W. W. Beman <beman>` and :person:`D. E. Smith <smith>` (:cite:`Boston: Ginn & Co., 1897 <klein:1897>`), the monograph inaugurated Klein's lifelong program of bridging the gulf ("double discontinuity") between university research and elementary school geometry.

Core Distinctions of the Introduction
-------------------------------------

1. **Practical Approximation vs. Theoretical Exactitude:**
   Empirical drawing (graduated scales, protractors, mechanical linkages) operates within bounded physical tolerances where every problem admits arbitrarily close numerical approximation. *Theoretical constructibility*, by contrast, is an exact deductive question: whether a magnitude belongs to the algebraic closure generated by finitely many ideal straightedge-and-compass incidences (:person:`Euclid <euclid>`, :cite:`*Elements* I, Postulates 1–3 <euclid:elements>`).
2. **Critique of Textbook Dogmatism & Tribute to Julius Petersen:**
   Klein observes that school geometries routinely assert the impossibility of doubling the cube, trisecting the angle, constructing the regular heptagon, and squaring the circle as unproved dogma—commending :person:`Julius Petersen <petersen>` (:cite:`1878/1879 <petersen:1879>`) as virtually the sole textbook author supplying elementary algebraic proofs.
3. **Analytic Translation & Klein's Fundamental Criterion (*Hauptsatz*):**
   Line–line intersections perform rational field operations ($+, -, \times, \div$); line–circle and circle–circle intersections solve quadratic equations, adjoining square roots $\sqrt{R}$. Conversely, fourth proportionals and semicircle altitudes construct every rational operation and square root. Hence **Euclidean constructibility is identical with membership in a finite iterated quadratic extension tower** over the :term:`domain of rationality <domain-of-rationality>` of the given segments.
4. **Bifurcation of the Treatise:**
   * **Part I (Chapters I–V):** Algebraic equations and constructible vs. non-constructible algebraic numbers (:person:`Wantzel <wantzel>`, :person:`Gauss <gauss>`, :person:`Eisenstein <eisenstein>`, :person:`von Staudt <staudt>`, :person:`Schröter <schroeter>`).
   * **Part II (Chapters I–V):** :term:`Transcendental numbers <transcendental-number>` (:person:`Cantor <cantor>`, :person:`Hermite <hermite>`, :person:`Lindemann <lindemann>`, :person:`Gordan <gordan>`) and continuous transcendental integration (:person:`Abdank-Abakanowicz <abakanowicz>`).
