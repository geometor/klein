geometor.klein.constructibility¶

Exact symbolic constructibility analyzer for Felix Klein’s Famous Problems (1895 / 1897).

Implements the algebraic criteria of Part I (Chapters I–III) and Part II (Chapters III–IV):

  1. Klein’s Fundamental Criterion (Part I, Ch. I, §§ 1–15): Every analytic expression constructible with straightedge and compasses lies in an iterated quadratic radical tower over its domain of rationality \(\mathbb{Q}\), so its irreducible minimal polynomial \(\varphi(x) = 0\) must have degree \(M = 2^h\). Moreover, for irreducible quartics (\(M = 4\)), solvability by square roots alone holds if and only if the cubic resolvent reduces over \(\mathbb{Q}\) (\(\mathrm{Gal} \subseteq D_4\), excluding \(A_4\) and \(S_4\)).

  2. Gauss–Wantzel Cyclotomic Criterion (Part I, Ch. III, §§ 1–8): A regular \(n\)-gon (\(n \ge 3\)) is constructible with straightedge and compasses if and only if \(\varphi(n) = 2^k\), equivalently \(n = 2^a p_1 \cdots p_r\) where \(p_1 < \dots < p_r\) are distinct Fermat primes \(p_i = 2^{2^{\mu_i}} + 1\).

  3. Hermite–Lindemann Transcendence Obstruction (Part II, Chs. III–IV): Transcendental constants such as \(e\) and \(\pi\) satisfy no algebraic equation with rational coefficients whatsoever, ruling out quadrature of the circle by any algebraic curve or compass-and-straightedge construction.

geometor.klein.constructibility.cyclotomic_real_minimal_poly(n: int, var: sympy.Symbol | None = None) → sympy.Poly¶

Compute the exact minimal polynomial over \(\mathbb{Q}\) of \(u = 2\cos(2\pi/n)\).

For \(n \ge 3\), \(\Phi_n(z)\) has even degree \(\varphi(n) = 2d\) and is palindromic, so \(z^{-d}\Phi_n(z)\) is an integer polynomial of degree \(d\) in \(u = z + z^{-1} = 2\cos(2\pi/n)\). For \(n = 1, 2\), returns \(u - 2\) and \(u + 2\) respectively.

geometor.klein.constructibility.cubic_resolvent(quartic_poly: sympy.Poly, var: sympy.Symbol | None = None) → sympy.Poly¶

Return the exact depressed cubic resolvent \(R(t)\) of a degree-4 polynomial.

Depresses \(a_4 x^4 + a_3 x^3 + a_2 x^2 + a_1 x + a_0\) via \(x = y - a_3/(4a_4)\) into \(y^4 + p y^2 + q y + r\) and returns \(R(t) = t^3 - 2p t^2 + (p^2 - 4r) t + q^2\) over \(\mathbb{Q}\).

geometor.klein.constructibility.analyze_constructibility(poly_or_n: int | sympy.Integer | sympy.Poly | sympy.Expr) → ConstructibilityReport¶

Analyze compass-and-straightedge constructibility of a regular polygon, polynomial, or number.

Parameters:

poly_or_n –

  • int / sympy.Integer (\(n \ge 3\)): tests the regular \(n\)-gon via Euler’s totient \(\varphi(n) = 2^k\) and Fermat-prime factorization.

  • sympy.Poly or univariate sympy.Expr: factors over \(\mathbb{Q}\) and applies Klein’s degree-\(2^h\) criterion plus cubic-resolvent Galois check.

  • Closed-form algebraic or transcendental sympy.Expr (no free symbols): detects transcendence (\(\pi\), \(e\)) or computes the exact minimal polynomial over \(\mathbb{Q}\).

Returns:

A populated ConstructibilityReport with exact minimal polynomial, tower depth, and Galois/transcendence obstruction diagnosis.
