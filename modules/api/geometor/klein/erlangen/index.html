geometor.klein.erlangen¶

Exact symbolic verification of Felix Klein’s Erlangen Program (1872 / 1893) group hierarchy.

Demonstrates Klein’s foundational principle (Vergleichende Betrachtungen, §§ 1–2, § 6, § 10, Note V): a geometry on a manifold \(M\) is the invariant theory of a transformation group \(G\) acting on \(M\). As the transformation group widens along the chain

\[G_{\text{Euclidean}} \subset G_{\text{Principal}} \subset G_{\text{Affine}} \subset G_{\text{Projective}},\]

and branches laterally from \(G_{\text{Principal}}\) into the geometry of reciprocal radii (\(G_{\text{M\"{o}bius}}\), § 6), exact algebraic invariants are progressively shed while deeper structural invariants survive. All calculations use 100% exact rational / symbolic SymPy arithmetic.

geometor.klein.erlangen.circular_quadrance_cross_ratio(p1: sympy.geometry.Point2D, p2: sympy.geometry.Point2D, p3: sympy.geometry.Point2D, p4: sympy.geometry.Point2D) → sympy.Expr¶

Compute the Möbius-invariant 4-point quadrance cross-ratio \(\frac{Q(P_1,P_3)Q(P_2,P_4)}{Q(P_1,P_4)Q(P_2,P_3)}\).

Under any Euclidean similarity or any circle inversion \(P \mapsto O + R^2(P-O)/Q(O,P)\) (whose center \(O\) is distinct from \(P_1..P_4\)), the squared chord length transforms as \(Q(P_i', P_j') = R^4 Q(P_i, P_j) / [Q(O, P_i) Q(O, P_j)]\), so all four denominator factors \(Q(O, P_k)\) cancel identically! Furthermore, when \(A, B, C, D\) are collinear, this coincides with the square \((A, B; C, D)^2\) of the projective cross-ratio.

geometor.klein.erlangen.verify_erlangen_invariants() → ErlangenHierarchyReport¶

Execute an exact symbolic invariant audit across four groups of Klein’s Erlangen Program.

Applies four rational planar transformations to a shared exact rational point configuration:

  1. principal_similarity (Hauptgruppe, § 1): rational rotation \((3/5, 4/5)\), isotropic scaling \(5/2\), and translation \((3, -2)\).

  2. affine_shear (§ 2): equiaffine shear + translation \((x, y) \mapsto (x + 2y + 1, y - 3)\).

  3. projective_homography (§ 3): nonsingular projective collineation \((x, y) \mapsto \left(\frac{2x + y + 1}{x + y + 3}, \frac{x + 3y + 2}{x + y + 3}\right)\).

  4. moebius_circle_inversion (Geometrie der reciproken Radien, § 6): inversion in the circle centered at \(O_{\mathrm{inv}}(-1, -2)\) of squared radius \(R^2 = 5\).

geometor.klein.erlangen.verify_cayley_klein_additivity() → CayleyKleinReport¶

Prove symbolic additivity of Klein’s projective metric \(d(A, B) = c \log(A, B; I_1, I_2)\).

In Note V (On the So-Called Non-Euclidean Geometry) and § 10.2 of the Erlangen Program, Klein subsumes hyperbolic, elliptic, and parabolic metrics under projective geometry by adjoining an invariant second-degree Absolute (\(\Omega = 0\)). Along any line meeting the Absolute in \(I_1, I_2\), the cross-ratio multiplicativity \((A, B; I_1, I_2) \cdot (B, C; I_1, I_2) = (A, C; I_1, I_2)\) converts into exact geodesic distance additivity \(d(A, B) + d(B, C) = d(A, C)\).
