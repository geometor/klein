Exact Symbolic Verification Over $\mathbb{Q}$ (geometor.klein.erlangen)¶

In geometor.klein.erlangen.verify_erlangen_invariants(), we instantiate rational test configurations inside geometor.model.Model (with zero floating-point approximations) and apply exact representatives of Klein’s four planar groups:

  1. Principal Group (§ 1 — Rational Similarity): $(x, y) \mapsto (3x - 4y + 2,\; 4x + 3y - 1)$ (Pythagorean rotation-dilation scaling lengths by $5$ and quadrances by $25$).

  2. Affine Group (§ 2 — Area-Scaling Shear): $(x, y) \mapsto (x + 2y + 1,\; 3y - 2)$ (fixes the line at infinity $w = 0$ without fixing the circular points $I, J$).

  3. Projective Group (§ 3 — Full Homography): $(x, y) \mapsto \left(\frac{2x + y + 1}{x + y + 3},\; \frac{x + 3y + 2}{x + y + 3}\right)$ (moves the line at infinity to a finite horizon).

  4. Reciprocal Radii (§ 6 — Möbius Circle Inversion): $(x, y) \mapsto \left(\frac{4x}{x^2 + y^2},\; \frac{4y}{x^2 + y^2}\right)$ (inverts across $x^2 + y^2 = 4$).

Philological Note on Footnote 8 (Group Inverses, 1893)¶

In 1872, following the finite permutation terminology of Galois, Serret, and Camille Jordan, Klein defined a group solely by closure under composition ($g_1, g_2 \in G \implies g_1 g_2 \in G$). For a finite set of bijections, closure automatically implies $g^{-1} = g^{k-1} \in G$; for infinite continuous families (such as contractions $x \mapsto \lambda x$ with $0 < \lambda < 1$), closure alone defines only a semigroup. Klein’s 1893 bracketed addition in Footnote 8 explicitly supplies the inverse axiom required by Sophus Lie's Theorie der Transformationsgruppen.
